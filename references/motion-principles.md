# Motion Principles

## Purpose First

Every motion decision must serve a UX purpose:

- Feedback
- Orientation
- Continuity
- Hierarchy
- Perceived performance
- Emotional polish

Do not add motion purely for decoration.

## Restraint

Enterprise interfaces should feel calm and productive. Keep animation subtle, fast, and legible.

## Consistency

Use shared durations, easing curves, elevation rules, and interaction patterns across the product.

## Hierarchy

Motion should make important content feel more present and secondary content feel quieter.

## Accessibility

Always respect reduced motion preferences.
