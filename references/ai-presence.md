# AI Presence Motion

AI-related UI should feel subtly alive, not noisy.

## Use Cases

- AI assistant sidebars
- Prompt input panels
- Recommendation cards
- Streaming responses
- Thinking states
- Generated insight cards

## Patterns

### Breathing Surface

Use slow, low-opacity glow or gradient movement on the AI container.

### Streaming Reveal

Reveal AI content progressively using opacity and translateY.

### Thinking Pulse

Use a soft pulse or flowing gradient rather than bouncing dots when the product style is premium.

### Recommendation Cards

Cards can enter with slight stagger and soft elevation.

## Avoid

- Robotic blinking
- Strong pulse
- Chat-app bounce
- Excessive typing animations
- Distracting loops
